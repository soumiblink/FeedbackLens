# FeedbackLens - Features Quick Reference

## 🎯 All Features at a Glance

### 📊 Dashboard
**Route:** `/`  
**What:** Overview of feedback analytics  
**Shows:** Total feedback, sentiment, top complaints, trends

---

### 💊 Product Health
**Route:** `/product-health`  
**What:** Single-view health monitoring  
**Shows:** Health score (0-100), grade (A-F), trend, KPIs, top risk/opportunity

---

### 📤 Upload Feedback
**Route:** `/upload`  
**What:** Import customer feedback  
**Accepts:** CSV files, manual text

---

### 📥 Feedback Inbox
**Route:** `/feedback`  
**What:** Browse individual feedback  
**Filters:** Sentiment, type (complaints/requests)

---

### 🎯 Prioritization
**Route:** `/prioritization`  
**What:** Ranked product opportunities  
**Shows:** Topics by priority, evidence, "Add to Roadmap" button

---

### 👥 Customer Segments
**Route:** `/customers`  
**What:** Understand segment experiences  
**Shows:** 5 segments (Enterprise/SMB/Education/Paid/General), health scores, comparison

---

### 🗺️ Roadmap Planner
**Route:** `/roadmap`  
**What:** Convert opportunities to plans  
**Shows:** Kanban board (Backlog/Planned/In Progress/Released)

---

### 📈 Release Impact
**Route:** `/releases`  
**What:** Compare before/after releases  
**Shows:** Sentiment changes, health score, topic changes, timeline

---

### 🧠 Hindsight Memory
**Route:** `/memory`  
**What:** Historical context from past feedback  
**Shows:** Memory timeline, batch summaries

---

### ⚡ cascadeflow Runtime
**Route:** `/runtime`  
**What:** LLM routing intelligence  
**Shows:** Model selections, routing decisions, performance

---

### 📊 Executive Reports
**Route:** `/reports`  
**What:** High-level summaries  
**Shows:** Latest insights, risks, opportunities

---

## 💡 Common Workflows

### Daily Check
1. Product Health → Check score
2. Inbox → Review new feedback
3. Prioritization → Top opportunities

### Planning Sprint
1. Prioritization → Review opportunities
2. Roadmap → Add items
3. Update status as work progresses

### After Release
1. Upload → New feedback
2. Release Impact → Compare
3. Product Health → Check trend

### Segment Analysis
1. Segments → View health scores
2. Click segment → View details
3. Review feedback → Add to roadmap

---
